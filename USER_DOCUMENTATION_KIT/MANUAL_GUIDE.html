Managing Offerings (Homas, Pujas & Consultations)

Offerings represent the sacred ceremonies (Homas, Sevas, Pujas) and Astrological Consultations that Shri Pradeep Nadig provides to devotees.

Figure 3.1: Offerings catalog displaying active rituals, consultation cards, category badges, and edit triggers.
1
How to add a new ritual or consultation
Click on Offerings in the left sidebar, then click the orange button labelled + Add New Offering in the top right.
2
Fill in the Offering Form
  • Type: Pick Service (for pujas/homas) or Consultation (for horoscope reading).
  • Title: e.g., Maha Ganapathi Homa or Vedic Horoscope Consultation.
  • Slug: A web-friendly address, e.g., maha-ganapathi-homa (lowercase with dashes, no spaces).
  • Cover Image: Click the Media Library button to select any photo you've previously uploaded.
  • Description: Explain the spiritual significance and benefits of the ritual.
  • Status: Choose Published (live on the website) or Draft (hidden while you work on it).
3
Save and Publish
Click Save Offering at the bottom of the form. It immediately goes live on the public website!
Figure 3.2: The clean Offering Form where you type in the details, select image URLs, and choose Published or Draft status.
04

Managing Workshops & Automatic Seat Capacity

Workshops are physical or online group events where participants sign up and pay online via Razorpay.

Figure 4.1: Active Workshops with batch capacities, prices in Rupees, and remaining seat monitors.
⚡ Golden Rule - The Automatic Seat Counter:
You don't need to manually calculate seats! Whenever a student registers and pays on the website:
  1. Payment succeeds through Razorpay.
  2. The remaining seat count automatically decreases by 1.
  3. When remaining seats hit 0, the website instantly displays "Batch Full" and stops accepting new registrations.
1
Creating a new workshop
Go to Workshops in the left sidebar and click + Create New Workshop.
2
Setting Dates, Price & Batches
Enter the Title, Venue Address, Start & End Dates, and Price in ₹ (e.g. 1500).
Under Batch Settings, enter the batch name (e.g. Morning Session 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM) and the Total Capacity (e.g. 30 Seats).
3
Save & Activate
Click Save Workshop. Devotees can now browse and book online!

Emergency Troubleshooting & Fixes

Quick solutions to the 4 most common questions or unexpected situations.

?
"I uploaded an image, but it shows up as a broken icon!"
The Cause: The image address may not start with https:// or is from a private link.
The Easy Fix: Always upload your photo to the Centralized Media Library (/admin/media) first. Click the Copy URL button and paste that address into your form.
?
"The workshop says 'Batch Full' even though seats are left!"
The Cause: The batch capacity number was set too low or reached zero.
The Easy Fix: Go to Workshops, click Edit on the workshop, increase the batch capacity number (e.g. from 20 to 35), and click Save Workshop.
?
"Devotee paid on Razorpay, but I don't see them on the dashboard!"
The Easy Fix: Go to Enquiries -> click the tab labelled Workshop Registrations. Every single paid registration with full mailing address, PIN code, and Razorpay transaction ID is recorded here.
?
"I got logged out unexpectedly!"
The Easy Fix: For security reasons, browser sessions expire if left inactive. Simply go back to https://pradeepnadig.in/admin/login, enter your credentials, and sign back in.
