# Tutorial 5: Spiritual Blog CMS & Article Publishing

## Overview & Objective
Learn how to draft, format, categorize, and publish spiritual blog posts, Vedic guides, and ritual news articles.

---

## Prerequisites
- Administrator portal access (`/admin/blogs`).
- Article title, category, author attribution, and cover image from Media Library.

---

## Spoken Narration Script (NotebookLM Narration)

"In this tutorial, we will demonstrate how to compose and publish articles using the Blog CMS module.

Click Blogs in the sidebar menu to view your article repository. The grid view displays all published posts with thumbnail previews, post titles, category tags, author details, and quick action buttons.

To compose a new post, click Publish New Post at the top right. In the editor interface, enter the Article Title—such as 'Significance of Sandhyavandana in Daily Life'—and verify the auto-formatted URL slug.

Assign the Author name—defaulting to Veda Brahma Shri Pradeep Nadig—and select a category, such as Vedic Wisdom, Ritual Guides, or Astrology News.

Next, pick a cover image banner from the Media Library. In the main content editor, compose or paste your full article body, utilizing paragraphs and headings for structured formatting.

Click Save & Publish Post to immediately publish the article live on your website's blog section."

## Common Errors & Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article URL slug duplicate** | Another article exists with identical title slug. | Modify title or add year/number to slug field before saving. |
| **Formatting distorted on frontend** | Raw HTML tags or invalid markdown copied from external text editor. | Use clean plain text formatting when pasting content into blog body editor. |
